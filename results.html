Legally-grounded, file-specific disputes move the needle.

    Most clients see initial bureau responses within 30–45 days of the first dispute round. The full 4-round Accelerated Audit runs over 120 days — that's the cycle needed to pursue removals aggressively across all three bureaus. Your goal and timeline affect how we sequence the strategy.
