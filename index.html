Common Questions About Credit Repair

What is a free credit analysis?+

A free credit analysis reviews your current credit situation — including your score range, negative items, and approval blockers — to identify what can be improved. Clean Path Credit uses AI-powered analysis to match your profile against thousands of similar cases and provide a personalized strategy.

How does credit repair work?+

Credit repair works by identifying inaccurate, unverified, or obsolete items on your credit report and filing disputes with the credit bureaus under the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA). Bureaus have 30 days to investigate — items that can't be verified must be removed by law.

How long does credit repair take?+

Most clients see initial results within 30-45 days after the first dispute round. A full credit repair cycle typically takes 90-120 days across 3-4 rounds of disputes. Results vary based on your profile — some clients see 40-80+ point improvements in that timeframe.

Is credit repair legal?+

Yes. Credit repair is fully legal and protected under federal law. The Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA) and Fair Debt Collection Practices Act (FDCPA) give every consumer the right to dispute inaccurate information on their credit reports. Clean Path Credit uses these legal frameworks to file disputes on your behalf.
